Set it up in Slack

  1. 1
    Copy the setup text above.
    Use the “Copy the setup text” button.
  2. 2
    Go to api.slack.com/apps and sign in.
    Sign in with the Slack account for your workspace.
  3. 3
    Click “Create New App.”
  4. 4
    Choose “From an app manifest.”
    Then select your workspace and continue.
  5. 5
    Paste the setup text, then click “Next.”
    Delete any sample text that's already in the box first, then paste. A preview/summary screen is normal — continue.
  6. 6
    Click “Create,” then install it to your workspace.
    If it asks you to review permissions, click Allow.
  7. 7
    Grab your two keys.
    You'll copy two keys and send them to us in the next step:
    • • Bot User OAuth Token — starts with xoxb-, under “OAuth & Permissions.”
    • • App-Level Token — starts with xapp- (usually the default app token), under “Basic Information → App-Level Tokens.”
    We'll point you to exactly where these live on the call.
